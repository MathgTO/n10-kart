Privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026 · Applies to the N10 app for iPad and Mac, and the N10 website.

Short version: N10 collects no data. Everything you import stays on your device.

What N10 does with your files

  • Session files you import (.xrk, .xrz or CSV) and any onboard video you attach are read and analyzed on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • N10 has no server that receives your data. The app works fully offline.

What N10 does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no crash-reporting services, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location or contacts.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information, because we don't have any.

Where your data lives

  • Your session library is stored only in N10's private storage on your device (and, on the website, in your browser's local storage).
  • On iPad and Mac, that storage may be included in your own device backup (iCloud or your computer) under your Apple account. We can't access those backups.
  • Session files that other apps hand to N10 (for example through AirDrop, the Files app or a share sheet) are copied into N10's folder on your device, which you can see and manage in the Files app.
  • Video you attach is chosen with the system picker, so N10 only sees the clip you choose.

Children and teens

N10 is used by junior karters and their parents. Because N10 collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children or teens either. There is nothing for a parent to request, correct or delete on our side. Everything can be removed on the device itself.

Deleting your data

Delete a session in N10 to remove it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. On the website, clearing your browser's site data does the same.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and in the app with a new effective date. Because N10 collects nothing, any change that would start collecting data would be announced clearly before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support link on N10's App Store page, or visit n10kart.com.
