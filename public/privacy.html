Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Applies to the N10 app for iPad and Mac, and the N10 website.

Short version: Everything you import stays on your device. The N10 app collects no data. The n10kart.com website uses Google Analytics to count visits. Your session files and lap data are never sent to it.

What N10 does with your files

  • Session files you import (.xrk, .xrz or CSV) are read and analyzed on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • On the website you can attach an onboard video to a session. It is only played back next to your data on your device. N10 doesn't analyze it, upload it or keep it after you close the page.
  • N10 has no server that receives your data. The app works fully offline.

What N10 does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites. (The website's Google Analytics is described below.)
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location, photo library or contacts. Files are chosen with the system file picker, so N10 only sees the file you pick.
  • No sale of personal information.

Website analytics (Google Analytics)

  • The n10kart.com website, including the browser version of N10, uses Google Analytics 4 (provided by Google) to understand how many people visit and which pages they use.
  • Google Analytics collects standard usage information: the pages you view, the referring site, your browser and device type, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and similar visit information. It uses cookies and similar browser storage to do this.
  • Google Analytics does not receive the session files you import, your lap times, telemetry, setup notes or videos. Those are read on your device only.
  • Google processes this information under its own terms. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • To opt out, block cookies for n10kart.com in your browser or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. N10 keeps working the same way.
  • The N10 app for iPad and Mac does not include Google Analytics.

Where your data lives

  • Your session library is stored only in N10's private storage on your device (and, on the website, in your browser's local storage).
  • On iPad and Mac, that storage may be included in your own device backup (iCloud or your computer) under your Apple account. We can't access those backups.
  • Session files that other apps hand to N10 (for example through AirDrop, the Files app or a share sheet) are copied into N10's folder on your device, which you can see and manage in the Files app.

Children and teens

N10 is used by junior karters and their parents. The N10 app collects no personal information from anyone, including children and teens. On the website, Google Analytics collects the usage information described above from every visitor; it does not include names, email addresses or session files, and we don't use it to identify anyone. Session data can be removed on the device itself.

Deleting your data

Delete a session in N10 to remove it. In the iPad app, this also deletes the copy of that session's file in N10's Imports folder. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. On the website, clearing your browser's site data does the same.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and in the app with a new effective date. Any change that would start collecting more data than described here would be announced clearly before it takes effect.
