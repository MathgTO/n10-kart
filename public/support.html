What N10 does

N10 is a karting data coach. It reads session files from your MyChron data logger (native .xrk / .xrz files, or a Race Studio CSV export) and analyzes them on your device. It shows your lap times, your best lap, where a slower lap lost time sector by sector, speed and RPM overlays, and setup observations from the data (exit RPM, gearing, clutch, tire pressure).

N10 has no accounts, needs no internet connection and collects no data.

Review your data between sessions. Never use N10 while driving.

How to import a session

iPad and Mac app

  1. Get your session file onto the iPad, for example in the Files app, iCloud Drive or by AirDrop.
  2. In the Files app, tap the .xrk, .xrz or .csv file, then Share → N10. N10 opens and imports it.
  3. Or, in N10, tap Import session, then choose the file (use Browse to find it in Files).

Website

  1. Tap or click Import session.
  2. Choose your .xrk, .xrz or .csv file, or drop it on the import box.

No logger handy? On the Home screen, Try sample sessions loads demo data (the iPad app also includes two real sessions recorded at Mosport Karting Centre).

Common questions

  • Some laps are greyed out. Out-laps, in-laps and partial laps (much shorter or longer than your typical lap) are never used as your best or compare lap, so they don't distort the comparison.
  • "Storage full, delete old sessions". Your device is out of space for N10's library. Delete a few sessions you no longer need.
  • A file won't import. N10 reads MyChron .xrk / .xrz and Race Studio CSV with lap, time, speed, RPM or distance columns. Email the file details and we'll take a look.
  • Deleting data. Delete a session in N10 to remove it from your device. Deleting the app removes everything.
